## Delivered contract

| Operation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `cases` | Sorted anomaly, revision and correction inventories; documentary subchecks; unique case/target pairs beside raw event counts |
| Open `assess` | Exactly five condition rows and fourteen fixed criteria, actual prerequisite bodies, currentness, scoped conclusion and capacity extent |
| Narrow `assess` | Existing finite regression conclusion, empty conditions, explicit `open_evaluation=not_applicable`, actual lint prerequisite |

Case selectors default to all exact-scope records; an explicit empty array selects none. Assessment conditions default to all five; an explicit empty array leaves every gate unassessed. Assessment executes prerequisites directly under the same request/run budget, without relying on separately scheduled requests. Required missing snapshot/cohort selections remain named gaps. Known claim/frame/snapshot conflicts also block support when optional scope anchors were omitted.

Inventory results retain `assessment=not_assessed`. Every assessed documentary/review subresult uses the common criterion envelope. Outcome subresults qualify the **stated objective and effective/ineffective result**: a supported record of an ineffective restriction remains an ineffective result. This prevents a documentary verdict from becoming an invented successful repair.

## Evidence interpretation within the existing grammar

The runtime checks explicit typed fields and bindings. It does not extract conclusions from prose. Known review method and rationale carry the supplied assessor's attestation about the named criterion, declared horizon and bounded inventory. Supplied receipts must have the exact scope, an appropriate evidence kind, a known producer, supplied access and known content. All named supporting receipts must qualify. Their `subject_ids` collectively bind the judgment to the required subjects; favorable text alone cannot substitute for missing anchors or witnesses.

Analysis receipts support the judgment criteria unless the table identifies a process or validation/result evidence type. All fourteen criteria retain separate exact claim/criterion/scope reviews. The same receipts may be referenced by different reviews, retaining their shared basis. A general favorable review cannot discharge a missing criterion.

An empty captured anomaly inventory is explicitly reviewed through the claim/frame accountability anchors. Existing exact-scope anomalies cannot be hidden by an empty selector. An empty known tail designation list is supported only as the reviewed bounded empty obligation; all three tail reviews remain required and an empty population cannot support retention. Neither empty inventory asserts global absence.

For partial/unknown external membership, member-bound gate receipts must explicitly anchor known qualified members and their actual use targets/events. Whole-cohort receipt anchors leave that stronger claim unresolved. The prerequisite preserves its known-subset counts and unavailable full denominator. This convention uses existing subject IDs and keeps the review's finite reach inspectable without adding a schema field or parsing prose.

Source reviews cover the captured scoped origin/transformation and evaluator inventories, including supplied frontiers and known limitations. No complete hidden ancestry or automatic independence requirement is added. Explicit independence records trigger the P1-3 exact member/dimension assessment as nested prerequisites, preserving overlap, gaps, unexamined dimensions and contrary records. Pairwise reviews are never composed.

## Anomalies, revisions and corrections

An anomaly's evidence pointer may name an original supplied record or an authorized retained representation. A pointer without accessible content remains an access gap. Preservation and recorded disposition are separate. `investigated_no_change` preserves the investigation decision without asserting closure. Declared recut/restriction dispositions require their explicit corresponding revision/case links.

A revision reports documentation, incorporation and reviewed fidelity independently. Documentation retains old/new frames, changed distinction, named original anomalies and scoped receipts. A declared recut needs a complete class map and a changed represented class definition, cardinality, feature or frame boundary field. Identical meanings with renamed labels remain `relabel_only`. Incorporation additionally requires a scoped uptake receipt and unambiguous admitted affected assignments with supplied item bodies under the new frame. Multiple new assignments for one item remain unresolved. Improved fidelity requires its own `structural_revision` analysis review bound to the revision and both frames, together with documented recut and incorporation. Reannotation adds no observation or model-recovery claim.

Correction receipts bind to their parent case. Handling, attempts, actions and outcomes keep distinct nested event IDs. An action names its exact attempt; an outcome names its exact action. A supported action requires known kind, target, time and resulting state, a documented attempt, the same target and ordered attempt/action times. Route, authority, attempt and action must agree on actor/target for authorized capacity. Authority covers the action in its half-open interval. An earlier submitted attempt may precede the authority interval; the actual action must fall within it. Unknown times cannot support the time-bound conclusion.

Outcome review selection is bound to the exact case/outcome/scope. The explicitly named review must qualify or have an exact qualified resolution; an unrelated favorable review cannot bypass it. Case-level contrary records and outcome-local contrary records are retained, with differently bound outcome reviews kept separate. Counterexamples/resolutions from another outcome or case cannot become a decisive defeat of this one. Qualified resolutions preserve the original opposing records.

Known objective, resulting state, criterion, protocol, horizon, assessment time, linked action and supplied receipts are mandatory for an outcome. Repair requires a separate supplied baseline receipt. Restriction/withdrawal may use a state check. Changing the objective to repair does not transform a restriction action. Unknown authority leaves a qualified outcome intact and blocks authorized capacity. An ineffective outcome can coexist with an operable route; a drill remains a drill throughout reporting.

Case-level capacity describes `tested_route`. Sustained assessment requires at least two distinct action times with qualified outcome records plus a separate process-history receipt dated no earlier than those actions. Repeated outcome reviews of one action do not create history. The exact capacity review attests the declared horizon and objectives. This remains supplied bounded evidence, without an extrapolation to indefinite future availability.

## Aggregation, interruption and reporting

A gate requires all its fixed criteria and currentness. Missing or expired validity prevents gate support while preserving completed criterion evidence. A decisive scoped contradiction takes priority and retains other positive evidence. All five supported gates, required claim fields, consistent selections, a known scope window and completed execution are necessary for an overall affirmative conclusion. Otherwise an assessed claim is unestablished; no assessed gates means not assessed. Unselected gates are never omitted or treated as satisfied.

Permitted affirmative wording is: **Supported under the declared scope, supplied evidence and ect-core/0.1 policy.** Reports retain the exact claim/scope IDs, selected prerequisites, limitations and contrary premises beside this result. There is no scalar score, certification, deployment authorization or evidence-authentication claim.

Nested work shares the existing one-million-unit request and two-million-unit run limits. Every completed child body returns to its assessment parent, including partial structural member decisions and partial case/event inventories. A limit during one prerequisite cannot replace the parent request with a child operation or discard an already completed decisive defeat. Whole-inventory pair/event totals remain unavailable on interrupted scans. If the serialized report cannot be delivered within the existing size/finding limits, the affected detailed basis and conclusion are dropped together.

The closed renderer checks the new shapes, five/fourteen row sets, aggregation, case/action/outcome links, authorization prerequisites, distinct pair/event counts, fidelity dependencies and partial states. JSON and Markdown share one report object. Known empty ID arrays represent missing actor/target identifiers; empty unexecuted subcheck objects occur only inside partial case rows. The existing serialization admits no nulls. Evidence bodies, locator strings, procedures, methods, rationale and resulting-state text stay in the dossier rather than being copied into reports.
