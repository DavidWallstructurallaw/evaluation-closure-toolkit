## 3 Resolved local meanings

### D1 Units and scope

The structural population unit is one **evaluation item version**. Keep its logical item identity, immutable version, assignment revision and snapshot membership separate. An assignment correction adds zero items. Reusing an unchanged version in another snapshot creates another membership, with no new independent origin.

Ordinary snapshots select one version per logical item. Multiple variants require explicit variant identities and lineage. A repeated membership reference cannot manufacture another observation. Identical content across distinct admitted items does not automatically merge them or establish independence.

Claims, model responses, judge decisions, evidence artifacts, input contributions, actors, process versions and structural classes are different units. One item may combine prompt, reference-answer and rubric contributions with different ancestry. A relation about one contribution cannot become a finding about every role.

Each operation names the components of scope it needs: claim/version, evaluated process/version, subject or contribution, frame, snapshot/population, time window, relation view and dependency dimension. This is a set of local requirements, with no universal tuple imposed on every record. Each report identifies its analysis time. Initial findings concern the captured dossier retrospectively; historical claims about what was known at an earlier release require an explicit evidence cutoff and supporting records. Full historical reconstruction is deferred.

### D2 Evidence and result dimensions

Keep the following questions independently visible:

- What is asserted, by whom, about which subject and time?
- What evidence type and origin support it, and was the supporting material supplied, merely located, protected or unavailable?
- Which claim-specific requirements were examined, satisfied, contradicted or left unresolved?
- What contrary evidence, identity ambiguity and coverage limits remain?
- Was the requested analysis selected, applicable and completed?

A derived finding retains every material premise's evidence basis. A path computed from declared edges remains a deduction conditional on those declarations. Artifact identity checks establish byte identity. A supplied `verified` label establishes no stronger evidence class by itself.

Missing, unknown, withheld, disputed, explicitly absent within a documented scope, inapplicable, unselected and unperformed remain distinguishable. An unselected dependency dimension is not a failed test. An execution limit cannot yield a clean negative finding. Known positive witnesses survive incomplete work elsewhere.

The assessment policy may specify claim requirements, assignment admission, review criteria, tail designations and comparison scope. It cannot waive evidence attribution, erase unknowns, redefine five-condition openness as a score, or promote simulation into observation. Presence of all required fields is a documentary completeness result.

### D3 Typed lineage and independence

Keep derivation, acquisition, training/exposure, evaluation, rubric/reference use, semantic influence and correction relationships distinct. Traverse only the selected permitted relation view. Citation, common ownership or the fact that two judges evaluate the same candidate does not automatically establish common acquisition.

Retain SIT's distinct questions about acquisition, analytical method, model ancestry, evaluation rubric and organizational control. Documentary qualification of a supplied independence assessment requires its exact finite member set, pairwise or setwise form, dimension, method, assessor, scope/time, supporting basis, unexamined dimensions and relevant contrary evidence. Incomplete assessments remain visible as attributed declarations with their gaps and limited qualification. Qualification remains documentary.

Different IDs or providers, disconnected components and no discovered ancestor cannot establish independence. Pairwise independence assessments are neither transitive nor a joint-set assessment. Known shared origin may coexist with missing ancestry. An unresolved frontier is not one counted hidden origin or a bound on hidden origin count. Any retained upstream root-concentration result preserves its declared population, allocation, coverage and scope. It does not estimate error covariance or independent validators.

A recursive-reuse finding needs a finite typed witness linking an earlier output or evaluative artifact to a later generation, training, selection, reference or judgment process. An acyclic versioned artifact graph can represent recursion across generations. A static graph cycle cannot establish that process by itself.

RIT's direct and lineage closure intervals answer different supplied-record questions. Neither interval is adopted as a native ECT openness or global closure score. A future adapter must retain the original record units and scope instead of relabeling the value.

### D4 External input and actual use

| ECT question | Relationship to SIT | Local meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Received | Closest to admission | This contribution entered the evaluated process/run. Uploading its file to the auditor is a separate event. |
| Retained | Closest to preservation | The relevant contribution or distinction remains recoverable at a specified checkpoint, with the preservation basis visible. Stored bytes alone do not prove semantic preservation. |
| Qualified | Assessment axis, with no one-to-one stage equivalent | The contribution meets the selected claim/use requirements under a specified policy and evidence basis. |
| Selected | Preserve SIT selection separately when supplied | A recorded choice to include or exclude a contribution for a named use. |
| Used | Bounded recorded-use aspect of influence | An exact link to a role, run, score, reference, judgment or revision decision. The claimed kind of use must be named. |

These questions do not form a mandatory monotone funnel. A supported use event may coexist with missing receipt logs or unmet qualification. Qualification may differ by use or be revised. It cannot retroactively qualify earlier use without an applicable historical basis.

Human authorship, remote storage, freshness and vendor identity do not alone qualify externality. RIT grounded carryover preserves earlier external ancestry; it does not create fresh independent corrective contact in a new evaluation window. Preserve origin, acquisition, recording and use times as different facts where relevant. No universal freshness interval is inferred.

Counts use a named finite contribution cohort, checkpoint and identity rule. Count unique members separately from repeated event records. Missing logs remain unresolved. Do not divide counts from different cohorts into a presence score, interpret record share as simulation lambda, or infer causal effect size from a use log.

### D5 Structural populations and arithmetic

Fix the selected item-version roster before assignment or outcome filtering. Declare scope exclusions separately. For a known selected population of size N, let A be the number with one admitted primary assignment and U the non-admitted remainder, preserving separate unresolved, unclassified, provisional, withheld and missing-record reasons: **N = A + U**. Known selected members with unavailable bodies remain in U with their own reason. If the roster itself is incomplete, do not fabricate N from the received subset.

Initial arithmetic uses a single unweighted hard partition. With admitted class counts c_i and A > 0:

`SCI = sum(c_i²) / A²`; `D = 1 - SCI`; observed support counts positive c_i; assignment coverage is A/N when N > 0.

The class distribution is conditional on admitted assignments. Unresolved assignments receive no invented class mass. A domain class with a label such as "unknown sensor result" can be meaningful under its frame; missing assignment data is never automatically recoded into it. Competing assignment claims remain disputed until admitted under the chosen policy. Soft or multilabel aggregation is deferred.

Item validity and evaluated-model success are separate. An optional valid-only view filters by a declared item-validity rubric, while preserving the all-assignment profile and each denominator. A failed model answer cannot silently remove the test item. Validity cannot fill a missing structural assignment.

Known empty admitted distributions have observed support 0 and mathematically undefined SCI/D. Unknown populations have unavailable full-population quantities. A nonempty single-class distribution has SCI 1 and D 0. Coverage is undefined for N = 0. This refines the handoff's general empty-population wording while preserving its prohibition on invented values.

### D6 Comparisons, tails and revisions

**Descriptive snapshot comparison** permits different population sizes when frame meaning, selection meaning, assignment policy, weighting and population view are compatible. It reports change between the supplied catalogs. Conditional subset arithmetic remains labeled and cannot establish whole-snapshot loss.

**Matched-cohort comparison** additionally requires an explicit finite roster of justified item-version pairs and complete coverage for the requested primary comparison. Equal counts or matching display labels do not establish matching. Missing counterparts cannot be discarded or replaced to improve the result. Partial descriptive views may remain available separately.

Pin the task, context, horizon, resolution, class definitions, validity target where used, and comparison policy. Initial direct contrasts permit the same frame or a reviewed semantics-preserving one-to-one relabeling. Record split/merge mappings without automatically projecting aggregate counts. A changed cut requires supported item-level assignments under a common comparison frame before comparable reanalysis. Reannotation creates zero observations and establishes no model recovery by itself.

Required/tail classes retain their prior designation, rationale and frame. Rarity, severity and performance remain separate. A zero admitted count means zero admitted sightings. Initial complete-snapshot absence/loss findings require complete membership and admitted assignment coverage under compatible meanings. Unknown members, missing snapshots or unresolved assignments block that negative conclusion. Positive observations remain usable.

A correction or frame revision preserves original records and invalidates only dependent conclusions. New class names or a larger support count do not establish improved structural validity.

### D7 Correction routes, actions and outcomes

Preserve six separate questions: route declared; authority for the action/target/time; case submitted and action attempted; documented case-linked change or removal; outcome evidence under a stated criterion; sustained capacity or effectiveness over the claimed horizon.

SIT supplies route, authority, submission/handling and linked-change meanings. ECT locally adds explicit attempt identity and claim-specific outcome qualification. One case can have several attempts or events. Acceptance is handling; a later version without a case link is not a documented remedy. Multiple events on one case-target pair do not create multiple corrected targets.

An outcome assertion names the triggering issue, objective, affected claim/version, action, resulting state, suitable criterion/protocol, assessment horizon, evidence and relevant contrary results. Comparative improvement requires an appropriate baseline. A removal can be supported without an invented after-artifact. A documented outcome remains visible when authority is unknown, while the authority gap limits capacity and authorization claims.

Capability repair, scope restriction, evaluator replacement and withdrawal are different correction objectives. Evidence that a restriction succeeded does not establish repaired capability. A drill can support the tested route's operability without becoming a resolved production incident. A functioning correction pathway does not guarantee that every attempted remedy succeeds. One successful case cannot establish sustained capacity.

### D8 Structural validity and reopening

Structural validity concerns whether the declared cut preserves the states, relations, boundaries, interactions, horizon and consequences relevant to its claim. The auditor inspects the supplied support and explicit mismatches; domain adjudication retains its attribution and limits. Reliable scoring, appropriate weights and a complete report answer different questions.

For a documented structural recut, retain the original anomaly or authorized retained representation, old/new frames, the distinction changed, supplied adjudication, affected assignments and incorporation/reassessment evidence. Distinguish the existence of revision, its use, and supported improvement in fidelity. Cosmetic relabeling supplies no consequential recut witness.

An anomaly can be legitimately investigated with no frame change; preserve its disposition without declaring closure. New reality-bearing cases can enter an adequate stable frame. Narrowing an unsupported claim is a correction of claim scope; it counts as a structural recut only if the represented cut also changes in a supported way.

EC's five-condition formulation governs ECT. SISD's four-condition presentation remains predecessor context. EC's claim-appropriate validation also resolves the human-identity issue: actor identity does not qualify evidence automatically. Claims about human consequences require evidence from the relevant people and interactions; other claims retain their appropriate proof, execution, observation or environmental constraints.

### D9 Five-condition claim assessment

| Required condition | Local assessment question |
|---|---|
| Structural validity | What supports the cut's fidelity to the stated target, and how are relevant anomalies preserved and examined? |
| External presence | What relevant contact outside the evaluated production loop is documented for the claim and time, and how does it enter the evaluation? |
| Source integrity | What provenance, epistemic type, transformation history, evaluator relationship, validation basis and uncertainty qualify the claimed use? |
| Tail retention | Which declared rare or severe conditions remain represented, with their results and gaps visible? |
| Corrective capacity | What authority and usable route connect discovered failure to an appropriate response, and what evidence supports the claimed extent of that capacity? |

For an explicit open-evaluation claim, all five remain applicable requirements. A decisive supported contradiction defeats the scoped claim. Any unresolved or unassessed required condition prevents affirmative support. Support under all five can establish only **support under the declared scope, supplied evidence and assessment policy**. Preserve supporting and contrary evidence together; partial execution remains visible even if one decisive finding is available.

An unselected condition cannot become satisfied or inapplicable merely to obtain a pass. No average, weighted total, percentage or minimum score can replace these meanings. Narrow regression/component claims may have narrower justified requirements and retain their supported value. They do not inherit the open-evaluation label or deployment certification.

### D10 Runtime independence and deferred choices

Use semantic reuse with small local implementations after authorization. Cross-tool adapters, common-package extraction and private-module imports are outside this initial mapping. An available public RIT or SD contract does not itself create an adapter requirement. SIT public integration remains blocked by its unimplemented public entry points; that blocks only that integration.

The optional finite resampling mode remains a P0-3 inclusion decision. Recommendation: keep the first auditor focused and document RIT as a companion unless an ECT-specific first-user workflow justifies a separate engine. Either choice must preserve analytical expectations, sampled paths and empirical dossier evidence as different objects. No simulation result qualifies external independence, actual reopening or effective correction.

Capture/replay, if selected, binds input identity, policy, frame, method, tool/schema and relevant numerical/runtime identity. A seed alone is insufficient. Replaying captured premises adds no independent confirmation. Exact byte replay needs an explicit supported serialization/version contract in P0-3.

## 5 P0 3 handoff

The selected semantic inheritance is ready for the minimal executable specification. No unresolved predecessor dependency prevents that preparation. The remaining work is bounded:

1. Encode these units, orthogonal result dimensions and reason-bearing states in the smallest dossier/result contract.
2. Specify the initial claim policies, admissible relation views, evidence requirements, compatibility checks and exact finding premises. Keep assessment policy inert data.
3. Select the initial supported comparison/revision subset and exact oracle fixtures; preserve explicit unsupported results for deferred cases.
4. Decide simulator and capture/replay inclusion, numerical serialization, input/resource limits, safe file/output rules, CLI exits and a small runtime matrix.
5. Prepare the first claim-lint vertical slice and its acceptance criteria for P0-4 readiness review.

Do not re-audit whole predecessor release histories to finish those decisions. Extend verification only for a distinct semantic, security or delivery failure mode. P0-2 introduces no extra ledger, approval registry or framework dependency. P0-3 has not been started in this delivery.
